# BAES Public Research Status — Ontology and Governance

**Document Status:** Public Research Status Record  
**Project Status:** Under Development

---

## 1. Purpose

This record provides a public-safe summary of a completed stage of BAES
research without disclosing restricted development material.

It records the current engineering status and the resulting boundary
for further development.

---

## 2. Current Foundation State

**Foundation Baseline v1.0 remains Frozen.**

The research summarized here did not justify a Foundation change.

No new Foundation-level ontology concept was promoted as a result of the
completed research.

---

## 3. Ontology Research Result

BAES conducted controlled research into whether additional conceptual
elements were required at Foundation level.

The current result is:

> **No investigated candidate demonstrated sufficient independent
> necessity to justify Foundation-level promotion.**

This result does not mean that the BAES ontology is universally complete.
It means that completing the ontology merely for structural
completeness is not justified by the available evidence.

Further ontology work is therefore **evidence-triggered**.

---

## 4. Authority and Governance Research Boundary

Authority and governance were examined as part of the broader research
program.

The current research result does not establish an independent
Foundation-level Authority or Governance ontology.

This does **not** remove authority or governance from BAES.

Authority, delegation, scope, approval, execution, conflict handling,
emergency handling, and related concerns remain relevant engineering
and governance subjects at the appropriate layers.

The distinction is important:

> A concern can be important to BAES without requiring a new
> Foundation-level ontology element.

---

## 5. Current Development Direction

With the current ontology research boundary established, BAES development
continues toward the governance and higher-layer engineering work needed
to turn the established conceptual basis into a reviewable standard.

The current direction includes:

* Governance Model development
* Evidence and validation development
* Specification preparation
* Controlled evolution
* Public / restricted boundary review
* Preparation for external validation

## 7. Disclosure Boundary

This public record intentionally omits:

* Restricted Foundation material
* Detailed ontology candidate analysis
* Internal research records

appropriate public state without becoming a mirror of the restricted
development repository.
