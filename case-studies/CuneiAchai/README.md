# CuneiAchai

**Case Study Status:** In Progress  
**Project Status:** Research / Development  
**Publication Status:** Public Case Study Record  
**Dataset Status:** Restricted / Not Publicly Released  

---

## 1. Overview

**CuneiAchai** is a research and engineering project focused on the construction of a structured, machine-readable corpus of Achaemenid Old Persian cuneiform material.

The project is maintained as a **BAES Case Study**. It is not part of the normative BAES specification and does not, by itself, establish or modify a BAES requirement.

The case study exists to document and evaluate the application of BAES principles to a real research and data-engineering project.

---

## 2. Current Scope

The project currently brings together, in a normalized structure, information associated with Old Persian cuneiform material, including where available:

- Cuneiform representation
- Transliteration
- Persian translation
- English translation
- Inscription metadata
- Token-level information
- Lemma
- Part of speech
- Morphological information
- Lexical meaning
- Descriptive notes
- Source references

The current corpus is a **development artifact** and should not be interpreted as a fully validated scholarly edition.

---

## 3. Current Publication Boundary

The public BAES repository documents the project, its methodology, data model, provenance, and validation approach.

The complete working/master dataset is **not currently published here**.

This boundary is intentional. Publication status and evidence/validation status are treated as separate concerns.

---

## 4. Relationship to BAES

CuneiAchai is used as a real-world case through which BAES can be examined in practice.

The intended relationship is:

```text
BAES
  ↓
Engineering principles and controls
  ↓
CuneiAchai case study
  ↓
Research / data engineering activity
  ↓
Evidence and findings
  ↓
Potential BAES improvement
```

A CuneiAchai finding does not automatically become a BAES principle or requirement.

---

## 5. Current Limitations

The project is incomplete and remains under active development.

The current state should therefore be understood as a documented development baseline rather than a final release.

Known areas requiring further work include:

- Source reconciliation
- Provenance detail
- Data-model refinement
- Linguistic validation
- Cross-source verification
- Consistency checking
- Release/version definition
- Publication and licensing decisions

No claim of universal correctness or completeness is made by this case study.
